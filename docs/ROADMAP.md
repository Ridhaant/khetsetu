# Delivery phases and priorities

Scope revised 25 November 2026. Nationwide reach applies to assisted intake and future discovery; delivery commitments grow only with verified regional capacity. Mumbai `400008` remains a default example. Historical checks do not become nationwide fulfilment evidence through this revision.

## Phase 1: Nationwide assisted sourcing

Accept buyer requirements and farmer availability from formatted Indian PINs through the existing Worker/D1 API, web experience and native assisted source. Preserve private receipt tracking, withdrawal and owner-only operational access. Explain manual feasibility review before submission and in status updates. Requests do not create orders, reserve stock or collect money.

Exit evidence: actual checks of valid/malformed PIN intake, idempotent persistence, private tracking, unchanged owner access and request/quotation rules. Document the commands and environment used; do not infer passing tests from the changed scope.

## Phase 2: Regional fulfilment capacity

Priority P0: maintain a state/UT and route readiness register, qualify real suppliers, establish produce/packing/transit limits, obtain delivery coverage and agree customer terms. Assign an accountable support/operations owner. Start with manageable volumes on approved routes anywhere in India; hold or decline infeasible requests clearly.

Priority P0: improve inbox pagination, notifications, monitoring, backup/restore drills and contact-recovery procedures as volume grows. Test Hindi and low-cost device usability before broader promotion. Native signing/distribution remains a separate release gate.

Exit evidence: approved route records, real supplier/quote evidence, measured operational capacity, regional compliance review and exercised support/incident procedures. National intake is not proof of universal delivery.

## Phase 3: Transactional marketplace foundation

Priority P0: wire and test the future production commerce repository; run real PostgreSQL/PostGIS migrations; prove concurrent inventory reservations and durable idempotency; implement real customer identity/sessions, private uploads/scanning and staff permissions. Complete Razorpay sandbox checkout, signed webhooks, refunds and reconciliation without demo roles or fake success.

Priority P0: verify reservation expiry/recovery, late capture handling, immutable order/fee/tax snapshots, invoices and category cancellation/dispute policies. Connect web and actual Android/iOS builds to shared authenticated account/order state.

Exit evidence: all transactional PRD acceptance journeys with database/provider/browser/native evidence, security review, restore drill and sign-offs for each activated service area. The retained Node demo and schema are inputs to this phase, not the hosted assisted backend.

## Phase 4: Configured online service areas

Activate real online ordering only for exact approved areas with eligible seller/provider coverage. An unknown or uncovered PIN can browse or submit an assisted enquiry but cannot bypass checkout coverage. Version route/fee/tax rules and monitor fulfilment, cancellations, refunds, disputes and unit economics by region. Extend live coverage deliberately as regional capacity is proven.

## Phase 5: Operational improvements

Priority P1: seller staff permissions, structured negotiation, repeat orders, moderated order-linked messaging, logistics adapters, richer reporting, preference-aware notifications and catalogue tooling. Add regional languages after user validation; improve media compression and offline drafts. Add distance/polygon serviceability only when operationally useful.

AI price forecasts, lending/credit, insurance, auctions, warehouse ERP and proprietary fleet operations remain excluded until explicitly approved with separate requirements and review.

## Decisions before commercial commitments

Confirm the legal entity and seller-of-record model, commercial terms, payment/settlement model for future commerce, category quality/cancellation policies, supplier/delivery capacity, regional support ownership/languages, data retention, signing/store accounts and cloud budget. Keep these decisions distinct from accepting a request for manual sourcing review.
